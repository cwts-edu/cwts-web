## 關於“陳道明牧師神學講堂”

</div>

這個始於 2021 年的年度神學講堂，旨在通過由神學院主辦，並與眾教會密切相關的高質量神學講座，搭建起一個鼓勵牧長、學者以及神學裝備者，在聖經、神學和實踐神學方面，與領軍的學者對話的平台，幫助教會的各項重要事工，以此加強教會與神學院在神國度裏的緊密夥伴關係，合一的踐行福音使命。

**關於2026 陳道明牧師神學講堂介紹:**
2026 陳道明神學講座——In Togetherness: Forging Resilient Family and Marriage Bonds Across Cultures，我們將們一起思考、探索如何在多元文化的處境中，建立健康、穩固且充滿生命力的婚姻與家庭。
本屆講座也象徵著陳道明神學講堂全新核心主題 Thrive 的正式開啟。未來三年（2026–2028），神學講堂將圍繞此主題，致力於幫助不同世代、不同文化背景的家庭與個人，在信仰、關係與生命中持續成長，活出真正豐盛的人生。
今年，我們邀請來自亞洲各地的女性學者與實務工作者共同組成講員團隊。我們非常榮幸有 Dr. Joey Fung 擔任主題講員，分享她對婚姻、家庭與跨文化議題的深刻洞見。
除了主題信息之外，您還可從五場精彩的專題工作坊中自由選擇，深入探討不同層面的婚姻與家庭議題。工作坊將由 Dr. Charis Geevarughese、Ms. Jenn Chen、Ms. Tania Choi-Chia、Dr. Eltice Lin 及 Ms. Shelen Ang-Ong 主講，結合理論與實踐，帶來豐富而具啟發性的分享。
誠邀您與我們一同探索 Thrive 的真義，攜手建立更堅韌、更健康、更充滿盼望的婚姻與家庭，在這個充滿挑戰的時代中，一起活出豐盛的生命。

**Join Us for the 2026 Daniel M Tan Lectureship!**
How do we build enduring marriage and family connections in an increasingly fractured world? Where do we begin? Discover the answers at this year’s lectureship: In Togetherness – Forging Resilient Family and Marriage Bonds Across Cultures.
This event marks the launch of "Thrive," our overarching theme guiding the Tan Lectureship through 2026, 2027, and 2028, dedicated to helping families and individuals flourish across generations and cultures.
This year, we are incredibly proud to feature an all-female lineup of Asian scholars and practitioners. We are thrilled to welcome Dr. Joey Fung as our keynote speaker. Beyond the keynote sessions, dive deeper into your choice of five transformative workshops led by Dr Charis Geevarughese, Ms Jenn Chen, Ms Tania Choi-Chia, Dr Eltice Lin, and Ms Shelen Ang-Ong.
Join us this September as we learn what it means to truly thrive!

**工作坊三： The Way Back Home: Integrating Identity and Asian Family (英文工作坊/ AI自動文字翻譯)** \
**講員: Tania Choi-Chia, LMFT**
<details>
 <summary> Tania is a psychotherapist, writer, and speaker... </summary>
 whose work sits at the intersection of theology, psychology, and Asian identity. Based in California, she brings over a decade of clinical experience in attachment, trauma, and relational healing, integrating modalities such as EMDR, somatic therapy, and depth-oriented psychotherapy.

Born in Singapore and shaped by cross-cultural experiences, Tania is especially attuned to the ways faith, family, and culture converge within Asian communities. Her work explores how theological ideas of love, sacrifice, honor, and obedience are often embodied within family systems – sometimes nurturing connection, and at other times complicating the formation of a coherent self.

Drawing from both clinical practice and spiritual reflection, Tania examines the role of “mirroring” as a deeply human and theological need—the experience of being seen, known, and reflected by another. She invites a reimagining of formation that includes not only doctrine, but the relational and embodied ways we come to know ourselves as held in the gaze of God and others.
</details>
